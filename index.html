About Project

Abstract

Medical costs vary based on age, lifestyle, health conditions, and insurance factors. This project analyzes a Medical Cost Personal Dataset using Pandas Profiling to find patterns in health expenses, patient demographics, and risk factors. The goal is to understand what influences
medical costs and how different factors contribute to higher expenses

Introduction

Healthcare expenses are a major concern for individuals and governments. Factors like smoking, obesity, age, and insurance coverage impact medical costs. By analyzing this dataset, we can identify key trends and correlations between medical expenses and personal health factors.

Literature Survey

Studies show that chronic diseases, unhealthy lifestyles, and lack of insurance lead to higher medical costs. Research also suggests that
predictive models can help estimate healthcare expenses based on demographic and lifestyle data. Data analysis helps in understanding
which factors lead to increased medical spending. 

EDA

EDA helps in understanding the distribution of medical expenses. Pandas Profiling is used to analyze:

• Age & medical costs – How age influences healthcare expenses.
• BMI (Body Mass Index) – Finding correlations between obesity and costs.
• Smoking habits – Identifying the impact of smoking on medical bills.
• Insurance coverage – Checking if insured patients have lower out-of-pocket expenses.
• Region-based costs – Understanding geographical variations in medical expenses.
• Correlations – Finding relationships between different health factors and costs.
